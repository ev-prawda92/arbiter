Why Arbiter

Most teams still resolve outcomes manually.

When money depends on whether something happened in the real world, operators still jump between contract rules, websites, spreadsheets, internal policy, and prior cases. That creates repetitive work, inconsistent judgment, and settlement risk.

Arbiter compresses that work into governed patterns and keeps AI in the right role: interpreting and explaining while deterministic controls preserve the binding decision boundary.

Control boundary

AI interprets. Policy governs. Evidence proves. Logic resolves. Humans handle exceptions.

AI / agents

Cluster, summarize, retrieve precedent, explain ambiguity, and recommend.

Governed core

Enforce authority, evidence, timing, policy, and deterministic YES / NO / HOLD semantics.

Human operator

Handle exceptions, approvals, and policy judgments that should not be automated.

Audit

Preserve who decided what, using which evidence and governing rule.
